Enquiry processing

How information submitted through our contact form is used.

The form sends your name and phone number or email address to Soyuzkhimpromproekt, together with your organisation and project outline if provided. We use this information to review your enquiry and contact you.

Your enquiry is stored on the website's server and is available to staff through a password-protected administration panel. Contact details are not displayed in the public sections of the website.

Do not include passwords, payment details or confidential project documentation in your message. Please agree a suitable method with the institute before sending project materials.

For questions about the processing or deletion of your enquiry, contact the institute: cxpp@cxpp.ru, +7 (843) 294-94-10.
