Industry engineering exhibition

12 April 2025

EVENT

In April 2025, the institute's specialists took part in an exhibition of engineering solutions for the chemical and petrochemical industries. The event brought together leading design organisations from across Russia.

The institute's stand

Our stand featured recently completed projects, digital BIM models and demonstrations of laser scanning and UAV photogrammetry. Visitors explored our full range of engineering services, from early-stage studies to design supervision during construction.

Conference programme

As part of the exhibition's conference programme, Chief Engineer Evgeny Lvovich Kilyakov presented BIM Design for Petrochemical Facilities: Practice and Prospects. The presentation attracted considerable interest from clients and partner organisations.

The exhibition enabled the institute to establish new business contacts and discuss potential cooperation with several industrial enterprises.
