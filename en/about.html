// WHO WE ARE

From the first idea to an operating facility

Soyuzkhimpromproekt is a general design contractor providing integrated engineering for chemical industry facilities. We support projects at every stage, from investment feasibility studies to design supervision during construction.

We use 3D modelling, laser scanning and photogrammetry, and provide scientific and technical support for high-hazard facilities.

The institute is part of Kazan National Research Technological University (KNRTU). We recruit talented graduates, help train process design engineers and keep pace with scientific research.

// HISTORY
1953

Soyuzkhimpromproekt Design Institute was established by a decree of the USSR Council of Ministers as a branch of a specialist design institute in Moscow. Its purpose was to provide construction documentation for chemical industry enterprises. In its early years, the institute designed auxiliary workshops and relatively simple core production facilities.

1957

The main production and support departments were formed. They remain the foundation of the institute's organisational and engineering structure today.

1971

The institute became an independent State Union Special Institute, Soyuzkhimpromproekt, within the USSR Ministry of Machine Building. It was designated the USSR's lead organisation for the design of industrial explosives production facilities.

While part of the Ministry, the institute carried out design work for more than 50 industrial enterprises and research institutes, acting as general design contractor for 26 of them. Clients were located in Ukraine, the Central Asian republics, the Urals, Siberia, the Russian Far East and the North. The institute also contributed construction documentation for projects in Iraq and Syria.

1990s

Following the dissolution of the Ministry of Machine Building and the collapse of the USSR, design orders fell sharply. Despite the economic crisis and the major contraction in construction, the institute's management retained its core technical expertise.

Drawing on its experience in hazardous and technically complex facilities, the institute began working with chemical and petrochemical companies in the Republic of Tatarstan. Regular clients included Nizhnekamskneftekhim, Kazanorgsintez, Nizhnekamskshina, Tatneft and Nefis Cosmetics.

1997

By a decree of the Russian Government, the institute joined Kazan State Technological University, now Kazan National Research Technological University (KNRTU), as a separate structural unit. The aim was to establish a research and design centre in the Volga region to develop and implement innovative technologies in chemicals, petrochemicals and oil refining.

Today

Soyuzkhimpromproekt is a multidisciplinary engineering organisation and one of Russia's largest industrial design institutes. It employs more than 500 people and delivers over RUB 2 billion of design work annually.

Current clients include SIBUR, Ammoni, TANECO, Tatneft, Nizhnekamskshina, Kazanorgsintez, Nizhnekamskneftekhim, Technodinamika, defence industry enterprises and other companies.

The institute has extensive experience working with international technology licensors, including Sinopec Corp. (China), FLUOR Corporation (USA), Tecnimont S.p.A. (Italy), Mitsubishi Heavy Industries (Japan) and China Chengda Engineering Co., Ltd. (China).
